{{.Personas}} synthetic personas in {{.Segments}} segment(s) each read the same {{.Variants}} candidate lines in a separate interview, presented in a rotated order so no line always came first. Each rated every line from 0 to 10 on clarity, relevance, credibility and appeal, picked the best and wrote a stronger line. The ranking and every score are computed from those ratings; the reasons, themes and suggested rewrites were written by a separate synthesis step over all answers ({{.Calls}} model calls in total).
