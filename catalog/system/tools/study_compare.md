Compare two finished studies of one audience and one kind: the verdict score, the answer mix (yes, maybe, no, would pay now, median price), the segments, which objections, needs and alternatives went away, are new or changed their share, and, for user interviews, how each person who answered both changed their own score. It asks no model and costs nothing. Present it faithfully: lead with the verdict score before and after, say how many people answered both and how many scored higher, lower or the same, name the objections that went away and the new ones, quote a person who changed their mind. The differences are from synthetic people asked twice, not a measurement of real customers; say so, and do not claim the change caused the difference beyond what the note of changes says.
