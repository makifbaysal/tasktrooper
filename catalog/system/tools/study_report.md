The report of a finished study: the executive summary, the verdict and purchase-intent index (0-100, computed from the people's own scores), the willingness-to-pay distribution and price tests, results by segment, the recurring objections, needs and alternatives with how many people said each, verbatim quotes, recommendations, the method and the disclaimer. Present it faithfully: lead with the verdict and what it rests on, give the numbers, name the top objections, quote people, list the recommendations, and always say the findings come from synthetic personas, not real customers.
