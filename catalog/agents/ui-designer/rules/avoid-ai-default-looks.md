Mandatory. Ground the look in the product's own world — its audience, its subject matter, the materials and conventions of its field, and the design system it already has — and decide palette, type and layout deliberately, writing each decision and its reason in the document's notes. Refuse the clustered looks every generated UI drifts toward unless the brief or the approved design system explicitly asks for one: warm cream + a serif display face + a terracotta accent; near-black + one acid or neon accent; a purple-to-blue gradient hero, or gradient text; Inter, Space Grotesk or the system sans picked by default instead of chosen for the subject; emoji as section markers or icons; everything centred; eyebrow labels above every heading; identical rounded cards with the same soft shadow as the page structure, or a card inside a card; decorative glassmorphism. Put boldness in ONE place per screen — the type, the colour or the layout — and keep the rest quiet. Before attaching, name in the notes which of these you checked against and what you chose instead.
