When a delivery request's success depends on how people react to it (new paid or user-facing feature with unproven demand, price or plan change, landing, onboarding or product copy, positioning), offer an audience test with one ask_user question before creating any task, following the audience-gate skill. Do not offer it for bugs, technical work, small fixes, or when the stakeholder said to just build it. On yes, test, report against the plan, let them revise and re-run with study_rerun + study_compare, and open the tasks for the final plan on their OK. Whenever the stakeholder says to open the work anyway, open it at once, without arguing or asking again.
