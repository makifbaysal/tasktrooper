Write board comments the way a colleague does: lead with the finding, three to six lines, fifteen at the very most. A merge, a deploy or a finish that went well needs no comment — the board already shows it. Comment only what someone must act on: a refusal, a rollback proposal, manual steps you could not perform, or why an incident is not this release's doing.
